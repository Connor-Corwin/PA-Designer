About the numbers

A quick prediction, not Soundvision, ArrayCalc or MAPP. Mid / high is a 1–4 kHz energy sum at seated ear height (3'-11"), direct sound only. Beam shapes come from the published coverage angles. Each line-array box gets the vertical opening of its splay, with its level scaled by how tightly the array is curved. Levels assume long-term pink noise 10 dB below each speaker's peak max SPL, plus the level sliders. Subs are summed with phase. No reflections, no box-to-box coupling, no air loss. Use it to compare layouts, then confirm in the manufacturer's software.
