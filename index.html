About the numbers

This is a quick prediction, not Soundvision. Mid / high is a 1–4 kHz energy sum at seated ear height (3'-11"), direct sound only, with smooth beam shapes from the published coverage angles. Levels assume long-term pink noise at 10 dB below each box's peak max SPL (A10i Focus 140 dB, Wide 137 dB, X4i 116 dB, KS21i 138 dB) plus the level sliders. Real arrays couple between boxes, and the room adds reflections, so use this to compare layouts, then confirm in Soundvision.
